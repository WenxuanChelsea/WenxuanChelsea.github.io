Wenxuan Li

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University, co-advised by Bloomberg Distinguished Professor Prof. Alan L. Yuille, and Prof. Zongwei Zhou. I am a member of the CCVL group. Previously, I received my M.S.E. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University in 2023, and graduated cum laude with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Delaware in 2021.

My research focuses on medical computer vision and AI for healthcare, with particular emphasis on building large-scale, well-annotated 3D medical imaging datasets and developing AI algorithms for early cancer detection.
